## Stride: kanban for people and AI agents

We also build [Stride](https://www.stridelikeaboss.com/), an AI-native work management system for teams that work alongside AI agents. People plan, review and approve the work. Agents claim tasks, build them and ship them. Everyone uses the same board and the same workflow.

- **Start from a real specification.** Stride turns a fuzzy idea into a requirements document, then into small, ordered tasks that carry acceptance criteria, the files to change, a testing strategy and security considerations.
- **People stay in control.** Agents only pick up work a person has moved to Ready, and any task can be set to stop for human review before it ships.
- **Quality checks agents can't skip.** Every change is reviewed against its task, and your own tests and linters must pass before it can be completed.
- **Bring your own agent.** Plugins for Claude Code, GitHub Copilot CLI, Gemini CLI, Codex CLI, OpenCode and Pi, plus MCP and a full REST API.
- **Free to start.** Free for individuals and small teams, open source, and free to self-host.
