## Helping teams adopt AI-driven development

Let's Tango works with companies to bring AI into how they build software. We help teams move from experimenting with AI coding tools to a dependable way of working, where people plan and review and AI agents do more of the building.

[See how we can help](/services/) or [get in touch](/contact/).

## Stride: kanban for people and AI agents

We also build [Stride](https://www.stridelikeaboss.com/), a work management system designed for teams that work alongside AI agents. People plan, review and approve the work. Agents claim tasks, build them and ship them. Everyone uses the same board and the same workflow.

[Learn more about Stride →](https://www.stridelikeaboss.com/)
