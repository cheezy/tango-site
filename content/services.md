## Consulting for AI

Practical advice for teams starting to build software with AI agents. We help you choose where agents fit in your work, set up the tools and checks that keep their output trustworthy, and work alongside your developers until the new way of working feels normal.

## Transforming your organization to an AI approach for delivery

Using AI agents well takes more than new tools. It changes how work is planned, reviewed and shipped. We help organizations reshape their delivery process around agents: clear requirements, small well-described tasks, automated quality gates, and people reviewing and approving the work rather than typing all of it.

## Half-day Stride onboarding

A hands-on half day where your team and AI agents build part of your proof of concept together in [Stride](https://www.stridelikeaboss.com/).

- **Start with ideation, not code.** We turn your idea into a written requirements document covering goals, assumptions, constraints, non-goals and success metrics. Most of the accuracy in agent-built work is decided before any code is written.
- **Turn requirements into tasks.** The requirements become a Stride goal: a set of small tasks, each with acceptance criteria, the files to change, a testing strategy and security considerations.
- **Watch the agents build.** Agents claim tasks, build them, and must pass your tests and linter before they can finish. Their work is reviewed for security and explored for problems scripted tests miss.
- **Keep going without us.** Your team leaves knowing how to take the next idea from requirements to shipped code on its own, and how much review the agents' work needs.
